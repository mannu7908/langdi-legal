Privacy Policy

Langdi Card Game · by ARHINA Games · Last updated: 26 October 2026

In short. Langdi is a card game for fun. Its coins are virtual only and have no cash value; they cannot be cashed out or exchanged for money, gifts or vouchers. You can play as a guest without giving us any personal information. If you sign in with Google or Facebook we store only what the game needs to keep your coins and progress. We do not sell your personal information. You can delete your account and data at any time inside the app (see Delete my data).

1. Who we are

"Langdi", "the game", "we" and "us" mean the Langdi Card Game published by ARHINA Games. For any question about this policy or your data, write to nametunebirthday@gmail.com.

2. Information we handle

a) Playing as a guest

Your guest name, avatar choice, coins, statistics and achievements are stored on your device. When you play online as a guest, the game server gives your device a random guest ID and secret token so it can recognise you at the table; the server does not receive your coins from your device except the balance your phone reports when you join a coin table.

b) Signing in with Google or Facebook

Sign-in is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) together with Google or Facebook. We receive your name, e-mail address, profile-picture link and the provider's user ID. We never receive your Google or Facebook password. We use this information only to create and protect your game account, show your name in the game and let you sign in again on another device. If the same e-mail address is used with Google and Facebook, both sign-ins open the same game account.

c) Game data in your account

  • Display name, avatar (or the photo you choose to upload), player ID and level.
  • Coins and a ledger of every coin change (free coins, entry fees, prizes, ad rewards, refunds, achievement rewards).
  • Statistics, achievements, time of your last free-coin claim, and results of online series you played (seat, declarations, scores).

If you upload an avatar photo it is stored in a public storage folder so other players at your table can see it. Do not upload a photo you do not want others to see.

d) Online play

For rooms with friends the server processes your moves, room code and the quick emoji / preset messages you send. Free-text chat is not supported. Rooms are deleted shortly after the series ends.

e) Technical information

Our hosting provider (Render) and database provider (Supabase) automatically record technical data such as your IP address, request time, app version and error messages for security and troubleshooting. We do not use this to build advertising profiles. If the app crashes it may also send a short crash report (app version, "android" or "web", the error text and a few lines of technical detail, but no name, e-mail or account data) to our server; these reports are kept for 30 days.

f) Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game may show ads through Google AdMob: rewarded ads (to earn coins or a prize bonus) and full-screen ads between games. Google may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where the law requires (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the game shows Google's consent message first, and ads follow your choice. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads on Android). During testing the game may show only Google's marked "test ads".

3. Why we use it

  • to run the game: accounts, coins, prizes, achievements, online rooms;
  • to keep the game fair and secure (for example to stop duplicate rewards and misuse);
  • to fix problems and improve the game;
  • to show ads that pay for the game (see 2f).

Other players at your table can see your display name, avatar, level and moves. The weekly leaderboard shows, to every player, the display name, player ID (or guest ID) and the prize coins won this week of the ten players who won the most; guest wins are reported to our server together with a random secret kept on the device. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the rights and safety of players.

5. Virtual coins

Coins, entry fees and prizes in Langdi exist only inside the game. They have no real-world monetary value, cannot be withdrawn, sold, transferred between players or exchanged for money, gifts or vouchers, and no real-money wagering is offered.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your account data while your account exists. When you delete your account (see Delete my data) your profile, coins, statistics, achievements and photo are deleted, your sign-in record is removed and your name is replaced by "Deleted player" in past game records. Our private backups are rotated automatically, so a deleted account can remain in a backup for up to 90 days before that backup expires. Guest data stored on your device stays there until you reset it or uninstall the game.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account or reset guest data inside the app (Profile).
  • Access or correct your data: your name, avatar and statistics are visible in Profile; ask us for a copy or a correction by e-mail.
  • Ads: use the consent message where it is shown, and your phone's ad settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK or India) you may have additional rights such as objection or complaint to a data-protection authority. Write to us and we will respond.

8. Children

Langdi is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to us and we will delete it. The game contains simulated card-game play with virtual coins; parents should decide whether it is suitable for their teenager.

9. Security

Connections to the game server and database use HTTPS. Coin changes are made only by our server, never by the app itself, and are written to a ledger. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

10. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours. By using the game you understand that your information may be processed there under the providers' safeguards.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
