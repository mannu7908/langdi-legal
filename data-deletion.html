Delete my data

Langdi Card Game · by ARHINA Games

If you have a Langdi account (signed in with Google or Facebook)

  1. Open the Langdi app and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile on the home screen to open My Profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm and tap Delete forever.
What is deleted: your profile, coins, coin history, statistics, achievements, level and avatar photo, and your sign-in record. Your name is replaced by "Deleted player" in past game records.
This cannot be undone. Our private backups rotate automatically, so a copy can remain in a backup for up to 90 days before that backup expires.

If you only played as a guest

Guest data (name, coins, statistics) is stored on your own device. Open My Profile → Reset Guest Data, or uninstall the app, to erase it. The server keeps only a random guest ID with no name or e-mail.

If you cannot open the app

Send an e-mail to nametunebirthday@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Langdi data", from the e-mail address linked to your Google or Facebook account (or tell us your Player ID, shown in My Profile). We will delete your data within 30 days and reply when it is done.

Using Facebook Login

Deleting your game account removes the data we hold. To also remove Langdi from your Facebook account, open Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, select Langdi and choose Remove. Removing it there does not by itself delete the data stored in the game, so please delete your game account first.

Ads

Advertising data used by Google AdMob is controlled in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID) and in your Google account.
